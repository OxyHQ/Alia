# Alia consumes central paid subscriptions

New purchases call Oxy's official authenticated SDK. `/billing/product-checkout`
only publishes explicit product/offer/version/plan/cadence bindings from
`ALIA_OXY_PRODUCT_CREDIT_ADAPTER`; it performs no financial write. Configure separate
monthly and annual offer references with `billingPeriod: "monthly" | "annual"`.
The central catalogue owns actual USD totals and availability. Existing seed prices
remain Go 399/3830, Pro 999/9590, Max 4999/47990, Ultra 9999/95990 USD cents
(month/year). Existing Stripe-paid/complimentary records and their management remain.
Mapped native plans refuse legacy financial writes. No local native billing mirror
or alternate entitlement authority is added.

The native individual plan can coexist with One. Central quota policy selects the
maximum eligible source and never adds overlapping allowances. Annual payment grants
monthly credit windows in Oxy; Alia cannot spend future months early. Allocation rows
retain consumption/reservation history. New windows receive separate immutable grant
IDs. Cancellation keeps already-paid periods; expiry of One keeps the individual
source; invoice refunds revoke every monthly window in the paid annual segment.

The subscribe UI exposes configured monthly/annual official checkout. Existing native
individual plans link to Accounts for source-specific cancellation or explicit FAIR
renewal, including when a larger One grant wins the credit maximum. Account/session
scoping fences reads. Durable idempotency keys survive lost replies. Redirects and
an older active plan only refresh data; neither announces a new payment succeeded.
